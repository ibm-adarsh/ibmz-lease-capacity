      How to read this

      • Prow cron times are UTC. Capacity comes from Boskos *-quota-slice name lists.
      • Each periodic is modeled as holding 1 lease for the assumed duration after each cron start.
      • low-frequency jobs (day-of-month / @yearly) are listed but excluded from the weekly heatmap.
      • Free slots with high spare capacity are the safest places to schedule additional jobs.
      • Dashboard refreshes daily at 08:00 IST from openshift/release when jobs, crons, or lease capacity change.
      • Raw data: jobs.csv · summary.json
